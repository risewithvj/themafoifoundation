Our Trustees

The Board of Trustees brings together diverse expertise in business, finance, HR, education and social development — guiding the Foundation with purpose and integrity.

HR

Hemalatha Rajan

Managing Trustee, The Ma Foi Foundation

Co-founder of the Ma Foi Group of Companies, Hemalatha Rajan has played a pivotal role in building the Foundation into a sensitive and strategic CSR arm. Her passion for empowering women has helped build a movement of change in the lives of over 50,000 women who now enjoy economic and emotional independence.

She is a Chartered Accountant with over 25 years of experience in Finance, Audit and Executive Search.

HS

Hastha Shivaramakrishnan

Trustee, The Ma Foi Foundation

Currently Principal at Tate Leadership Consulting Pvt Ltd, Hastha has been with the organisation for over 20 years handling various critical roles. She served as Chief Executive of the Executive Search business of Randstad. Hastha strongly believes in the capacity of every individual to give back to society.

Her belief in the importance of education has motivated many Ma Foi members to provide career guidance and mentoring to underprivileged children. She holds a Master's Degree in English and has graduated in Sociology.

AN

Aditya Narayan Mishra

Trustee, The Ma Foi Foundation · CEO, CIEL HR Services

Chief Executive Officer of CIEL HR Services, Aditya has handled the operations of various large centres in Ma Foi and Randstad. His commitment to give back to society has seen him involved in the conceptualisation of various Foundation activities. As Trustee, he helps run fund-raising and awareness campaigns.

Mishra holds a Bachelor's degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering and an MBA from Jadavpur University.

SK

Santhosh Kumar

Trustee, The Ma Foi Foundation · COO, CIEL HR Services

Santosh anchors operations at CIEL. An MBA with 24 years of experience, 18+ of which are in the HR Services industry, he has grown businesses manifold and penetrated deep into Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities in India. He has integrated staffing businesses of three companies into one unified entity.

Under his leadership, a Shared Services Centre was set up to drive efficiencies and standardisation across the merged entity.

RS

Rowena Scurville

Trustee, The Ma Foi Foundation

Rowena has over 20 years of experience in IT/ITES, Educational and Consulting industries, having worked with large organisations like IVL GmbH and US Technologies. She was a founding team member of Great Lakes Institution of Management, mentored by Dr. Bala V Balachander and later by Mr. Pandiarajan.

Her experience spans leading teams, setting up systems and processes, and engaging in strong student development initiatives.
