Mission &
Values

Empowering families through an integrated holistic development of their education, health and livelihoods — in collaboration with people and organisations.

Our Mission

"Empowering families through an integrated holistic development of their education, health and livelihoods in collaboration with people and organisations."

Every activity, every programme, every partnership of The Ma Foi Foundation flows from this single, unifying purpose.

Our Core Values

Freedom

TMF will always strive to create an atmosphere where employees and beneficiaries experience freedom of operation, choice, increased capability, decision-making and control over their lives.

Collaboration

TMF, wherever possible, will look for collaborating and working with people and organisations, building collective action and shared strength in pursuit of social impact.

Inclusiveness

The Foundation designs projects specifically to suit the requirements of marginalised and vulnerable groups, encouraging diversity in all its beneficiaries and operations.

Respect for the Individual

Every stakeholder is respected as an individual regardless of their lifestyle, preference, caste or creed. The Foundation believes dignity is not earned — it is inherent.

Dignity

Stakeholders will feel equal in their relationship with the organisation and with other stakeholders. The Foundation will never allow marginalisation within its own work.

Trust & Pro-People

In every relationship, the organisation makes efforts to communicate trust visibly. It will always listen to beneficiaries' needs and opinions, putting their interest first in all decisions.
