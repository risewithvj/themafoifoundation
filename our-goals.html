Our Goals

The Ma Foi Foundation pursues long-term systemic change — addressing the root causes of poverty through education, health and livelihood.

Universal Education Access

Ensure every child from a disadvantaged background has access to quality education — from primary school through college — supported by scholarships, coaching and leadership development. Break the cycle of poverty through learning.

Comprehensive Child Healthcare

Eliminate preventable disease and mortality among underprivileged children through the EKAM model — regular screening, specialist referrals, infrastructure investment in government hospitals, and community health volunteer networks.

Women's Financial Independence

Organise every woman in the Foundation's reach into a self-help group — providing microfinance, vocational training and entrepreneurship support to ensure they can stand financially independent and support their families.

Sector Collaboration Through CIOSA

Build the CIOSA network into India's strongest platform for NGO-corporate-government collaboration — enabling knowledge sharing, capacity building and collective action on social causes that no single organisation can solve alone.

Long-Term Vision

"A society where every child has access to education, every family has access to healthcare, and every woman has the ability to stand financially independent."
