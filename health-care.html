Health Care —
EKAM

Ekam Foundation is an NGO incubated and supported by The Ma Foi Foundation — bringing comprehensive healthcare to underprivileged children across Chennai.

About Ekam

Comprehensive Care for Every Child

Ekam Foundation brings together paediatricians, pharmaceutical companies, government bodies and hospitals to provide high quality, comprehensive and continuous healthcare to children who cannot afford specialist care.

Nutritionists, social workers and yoga instructors from across Chennai work alongside medical professionals to ensure underprivileged children have consistent and immediate access to healthcare.

Medical Care & Screening

To reduce prenatal and neonatal mortality rates, Ekam screens children in orphanages, anganwadis, schools and communities regularly. This involves timely identification of health problems, referrals to hospitals, and constant follow-up and check-ups.

In cases where children are hospitalised, Ekam takes complete care and offers the necessary financial support. So far more than 1,500 children have been supported through this model.

Training & Community Development

Ekam trains community volunteers from schools, nurse groups, NGOs and SHGs in basic healthcare and emergency care — to identify vulnerable children and build healthy habits. Training on alternative medicines is also provided.

Ekam's School Health Project targets school-going children aged 5–17 in a joint venture with the Corporation of Chennai and the Directorate of Public Health.

Government Hospital Partnership

EKAM works with government hospitals through a Public Private Partnership model jointly with the Institute of Child Health to improve access to quality-assured services. EKAM has invested more than INR 1.5 Crores in infrastructure, manpower and life-saving equipment.

Under a TN Government partnership, EKAM recruited 350 nurses in all district headquarter hospitals — reducing neonatal deaths in NICUs significantly.

Ma Foi Smart Centre — Avadi

In partnership with Tech Mahindra Foundation, the Foundation started a skill development centre at Avadi — training unemployed youth in business communication skills and workplace readiness over a 3-month programme followed by placement support in industry.

The training is fully supported by Tech Mahindra Foundation and has helped young people from the Avadi area access employment in the formal sector.
