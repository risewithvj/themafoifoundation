Our Story

Bridging Ambition & Opportunity

In 1996, seeing child labourers toiling in firework and match factories in Sivakasi and Virudhunagar, the Sornammal Educational Trust was born with a singular focus — providing children access to education and restoring their childhood.

The initiative extended to Chennai, identifying children with the courage to face life's odds and realise their dreams. In 2006, The Ma Foi Foundation was formally registered as the CSR arm of Ma Foi, bringing the Sornammal Scholar Scheme under its banner and renaming it DISHA.

Today the Foundation is a trusted implementation partner for corporates and government institutions — with three flagship pillars: DISHA (education), EKAM (health) and SORNAM (livelihood for women).

Our Purpose

What We Stand For

The Ma Foi Foundation was established with the belief that empowerment of the individual creates ripple effects across entire families and communities. Our programs are designed to address the root causes of poverty through education, health and livelihood — not just symptoms.

The CIOSA (Confederation of Indian Organizations for Service and Advocacy) was also initiated to serve as a facilitative platform for NGOs, companies and individuals for mutual learning and the collective espousal of social causes. Over 200 individuals, corporates and NGOs are part of this network today.

Milestones

Key Achievements

  • 3,800+ children supported under DISHA scholarships
  • 4,000+ children supported via EKAM healthcare
  • INR 30 Crores in microfinance loans disbursed
  • 200+ NGOs in CIOSA network
