Our Story

Bridging the Gap Between Potential and Opportunity

The journey of The Ma Foi Foundation began in 1996 as the Sornammal Educational Trust, with a mission to restore hope and opportunity through education for children affected by child labour in Virudhunagar. In 2006, the organisation evolved into The Ma Foi Foundation, expanding its vision to create sustainable social impact across communities.

Today, The Ma Foi Foundation is the CSR arm of the CIEL HR Group, partnering with corporates, governments, academic institutions, and civil society organisations to design and implement impactful CSR initiatives across India. Our work focuses on skill development, employability, women empowerment, higher education, digital inclusion, and livelihood enhancement, enabling individuals and communities to build brighter, self-reliant futures.

Driven by purpose and powered by partnerships, we transform CSR investments into measurable, sustainable impact.

Our Purpose

What We Stand For

At The Ma Foi Foundation, we believe that opportunity has the power to transform lives. Our purpose is to empower individuals and communities through initiatives that improve access to education, employability, entrepreneurship, women's empowerment, digital inclusion, and sustainable livelihoods.

We design and implement programmes that address real societal challenges, creating opportunities for people to realise their potential and build self-reliant futures. Every initiative is developed with a focus on long-term impact, measurable outcomes, and community transformation, made possible through meaningful partnerships with corporates, institutions, governments, and like-minded organisations.

Milestones

Key Achievements

  • Empowering thousands of youth and women through skill development and employability initiatives.
  • Enabling access to higher education through scholarships and academic support programmes.
  • Establishing Centres of Excellence, AI & Digital Learning Labs, and future-ready skilling initiatives.
  • Creating sustainable livelihood opportunities through industry-aligned training and entrepreneurship development.
  • Building strong partnerships with corporates, educational institutions, government bodies, and development organisations to scale impact across communities.
Our Vision

Holistic Community Development

We envision a future where every individual has the opportunity to learn, earn, and thrive. We strive to strengthen communities by fostering education, enhancing employability, promoting innovation, supporting entrepreneurship, empowering women, and enabling inclusive economic growth. Together, we are building a future where potential is nurtured, aspirations are realised, and communities flourish.
