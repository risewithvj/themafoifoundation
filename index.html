Our Story

Bridging the Gap Between Struggle and Opportunity

In 1996, seeing child labourers toiling in firework factories in Virudhunagar, the Sornammal Educational Trust was born — with one singular goal: restore childhood through education. That seed grew into The Ma Foi Foundation, formally established in 2006 as the CSR arm of Ma Foi.

Today the Foundation reaches 60,000+ women through self help groups, supports 8,000+ children through DISHA scholarships, and runs EKAM — a community pediatrics initiative providing healthcare to underprivileged children across Chennai.

Our Three Pillars

What We Do

Three focused verticals — Education, Health and Livelihood — form the backbone of all Foundation activities. Each pillar addresses a specific dimension of poverty and human development.

Education — DISHA

Scholarships, leadership programmes, sports training and village education centres ensuring children from disadvantaged backgrounds reach their full potential.

Health Care — EKAM

A community pediatrics NGO incubated by the Foundation, bringing together 100+ paediatricians, hospitals and nutritionists to provide comprehensive healthcare to children who cannot afford specialist care.

Livelihood — SORNAM

Microfinance, vocational training and entrepreneurial support for women clustered into self-help groups — creating financial independence one family at a time.
