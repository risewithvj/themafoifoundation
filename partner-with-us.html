Partner With Us

When organisations share their strengths they can do wonders. We invite partners in change who have a commitment to empowering children and women.

Why Partner

Your Partnership, Maximum Impact

Partnering with us will ensure you reach the right cause, the right people and achieve 100% utility of your donation. The donation will not be spent on overheads — and the Foundation will also share project costs.

You can donate financially, share your skills and expertise, or simply be present to support the communities we serve.

Financial Donation

100% of your contribution directly funds programmes — no overhead deductions. CSR-compliant receipts provided.

Skills & Expertise

Share your professional skills — training, legal, HR, technology, marketing — with the Foundation and its beneficiaries.

Volunteering

Be present — mentor students, participate in health camps, or engage with women's groups. Time is as valuable as money.

CSR Partnership

A structured, multi-year CSR implementation partnership with full reporting, impact measurement and recognition.
