Women Empowerment
Programs

When you empower a woman, you empower the entire family. The Foundation works with women groups on three platforms: Micro Finance, Vocational Training and Entrepreneurial Support.

About the Program

Self Help Groups — Strength in Numbers

To improve the livelihoods of women and give them financial independence, Self Help Groups were initiated. Women are clustered into groups and exposed to various vocational training programmes that help them start their own businesses or gain employment.

Insurance coverage is also provided to women groups under the Janasri Bima Yojana (LIC). 3,562 people are covered — each member contributes INR 75 and the Foundation contributes INR 25 per member.

Micro Finance

Each woman makes a small saving of INR 100–200 per month. The total savings across all groups exceed INR 0.15 million every month. In the last year, savings totalled INR 15 million and total credit availed was INR 35 million. Members also avail educational loans for their children (INR 8,000–12,000 per child).

Vocational Training

Women are exposed to diverse vocational training programmes — from tailoring and food processing to digital literacy. The Foundation also runs "Skillz on Wheelz," a mobile digital literacy programme bringing training to women's doorsteps across Tamil Nadu.

0 Women Trained

Entrepreneurial Support

Women are encouraged to use loans and training to start their own micro-enterprises. Market linkage support, business mentoring and peer group encouragement help women transition from employees to entrepreneurs — creating economic independence for families.
