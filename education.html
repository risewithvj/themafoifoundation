DISHA

Disha is an opportunity for children from disadvantaged backgrounds to believe in their strengths and rise beyond all odds — to become grounded, educated and sensitive individuals of the society.

About Disha

Beyond Education — Holistic Development

Disha is not just about education for children. It involves the holistic development of every child — allowing them to discover their hidden talents, encouraging them to believe in themselves and never let go of their dreams.

The programme creates an environment for children to learn, act, lead and grow into concerned individuals who contribute back to their communities.

Disha Scholarships

Provided to disadvantaged children to ensure they continue their education without financial hurdles. The initiative has supported over 3,800 children in Tamil Nadu with the aim of helping them pursue higher studies and gain employment.

  • 250+ have successfully completed college and are working today
  • 750+ children have reached college because of Foundation support
  • Covers Tamil Nadu with a focus on first-generation learners

Disha Leadership Programme

Provides children an environment to learn, act and lead so they can hone their skills, be productive and grow as concerned leaders. Disha Scholars from 8th standard onward undergo this programme through college.

  • 2,000+ children benefited by this programme every year
  • 13 schools have Disha Leadership Clubs run by students
  • Disha Youth Movement — scholars volunteer to help younger batches

Disha Sports Academy

The academy identifies potential sports talent between 10 and 14 years old and provides training and facilities to enable them to become professional sports persons. Children receive coaching in football, boxing and athletics.

  • 120 students trained in football, boxing and athletics
  • 3 National medals in boxing; State champions twice in 3 years
  • 120 total medals won across sports in the last 3 years
0 Medals Won

Gems of Disha

Focuses on helping academically weaker students with necessary study tools and exposing them to additional coaching classes at special supplementary centres — ensuring no child is left behind.

  • 250 students have passed 10th standard and are continuing studies
  • 500+ students studying in Gems of Disha supplementary centres

Village Education & Resource Centres

These centres are run in villages of Virudhunagar district where both parents are employed and children have no support system after school hours. The centres provide children with a safe, structured environment to learn and grow.
