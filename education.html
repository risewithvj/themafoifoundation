Centres of Excellence (CoE)

State-of-the-art Centres of Excellence established in partnership with corporates and academic institutions, providing students and youth with hands-on access to cutting-edge technologies and industry-relevant training environments.

Our CoEs include AI Labs, Digital Learning Labs, EV Labs, and sector-specific skilling hubs — creating future-ready talent pipelines for leading industries across India.

NextGen Tech Skilling Programs

Future-focused skilling in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and emerging digital technologies — preparing youth for the jobs of tomorrow.

  • Industry-aligned curriculum designed with leading tech companies
  • Hands-on training in AI Labs and Digital Learning Labs
  • Placement support into sustainable careers in the tech sector

Banking, Financial Literacy & Skilling Programs

Empowering youth with the knowledge and skills for careers in banking, insurance, fintech, and financial services — combined with financial literacy training that builds long-term economic resilience.

  • BFSI sector-aligned training modules
  • Collaboration with leading banks and financial institutions
  • Certification programmes with industry recognition

Accounting & Commerce Skilling Programs

Comprehensive skilling in accounting, taxation, commerce operations, and business finance — creating skilled professionals for India's growing financial and business services sector.

  • GST, Tally, and advanced accounting training
  • Commerce fundamentals through industry-ready certification
  • Employability support for placement in accounting and finance roles

Manufacturing & Engineering Skilling Programs

Hands-on technical skilling for youth in manufacturing processes, industrial automation, CNC operations, welding, fabrication, and engineering trades — building a skilled workforce for India's industrial growth.

  • Sector-specific training aligned with manufacturing industry needs
  • Partnerships with leading automotive and industrial companies
  • On-the-job training and apprenticeship opportunities

Logistics & Service Tech Skilling Programs

Skilling youth for careers in logistics, supply chain management, warehousing, retail operations, and service technology — equipping them for India's fast-growing service and logistics sector.

  • Industry-aligned training in logistics and supply chain operations
  • Digital skills for service technology and retail management
  • Placement support with leading logistics and e-commerce companies

Women Empowerment Programs

Holistic programmes that enable women through employability training, entrepreneurship development, digital literacy, and livelihood enhancement — fostering financial independence and self-reliance in communities.

  • Vocational and employability training tailored for women
  • Entrepreneurship development and business mentorship support
  • Digital inclusion and financial literacy programmes

Community Health & Environmental Programs

Programmes that strengthen community well-being through health awareness, preventive care initiatives, environmental sustainability, and support for underserved populations — creating healthier, more resilient communities.

  • Health awareness and preventive care camps in underserved communities
  • Environmental sustainability and green initiatives
  • Partnerships with health institutions and government bodies

Scholarship & Education Support Programs

Connecting students from disadvantaged backgrounds to higher education through scholarships, academic support, career guidance, and mentorship — turning aspirations into achievements for first-generation learners.

  • Scholarship support for higher education across India
  • Academic coaching, mentoring, and career guidance
  • 8,000+ students supported through scholarship initiatives
