Our Journey

From restoring hope through education in 1996 to empowering 100,000+ lives across India — a timeline of purpose, partnerships, and lasting social impact.

1996 — 2006

The Early Years

1996

Sornammal Educational Trust

Seeing child labourers in firework and match factories in Sivakasi and Virudhunagar, the Sornammal Educational Trust was set up. Children are removed from factories and sent to school. The Sornammal Scholar Scheme is conceived to provide access to education and improve self-esteem.

Early 2000s

Expansion to Chennai

The initiative extends to Chennai. DISHA continues to identify children with the courage to face life's odds and realise their dreams. Scholarships ensure underprivileged children continue to go to school, with many joining professional courses and degree programmes.

2004

Sornammal Matriculation School

St. Anne's Primary School in Chennai — on the brink of closure — is revived by the Foundation and renamed 'Sornammal Matriculation School'. It becomes the most affordable school in the neighbourhood with tremendous infrastructure development.

2005

Project Sornam — Women Empowerment

Ma Foi Foundation decides to look at holistic community development. Project Sornam is set up to empower women. Sornam Self Help Groups are formed, ensuring women can stand financially independent. Today 60,000+ women are part of this group.

2006

The Ma Foi Foundation Registered

The Ma Foi Foundation is formally registered as the CSR arm of Ma Foi. It brings the Sornammal Scholar Scheme under its banner and renames it DISHA. CIOSA (Confederation of Indian Organizations for Service and Advocacy) is also initiated — over 200 NGOs, corporates and individuals join.

2007 — Present

Growth & Impact

2008

Joy Centre — Vocational Training

A vocational training centre is set up. Joy Centre begins training for women and youth to strengthen and enhance their employability and marketable skills, bringing hands-on skill development to Chennai communities.

2009

EKAM — Community Paediatrics

An unique community paediatrics initiative providing holistic healthcare for children aged 0–17. 100 paediatricians and hospitals come together to provide high-quality, comprehensive, continuous healthcare to children who cannot afford specialist services. EKAM is later registered as a separate organisation.

2010

Disha Sports Academy

Foundation launches the Disha Sports Academy to train underprivileged children aged 10–14 in football, athletics and boxing — with the aim to make them national champions. Gems of Disha is also launched across 4 centres for academically weaker students.

2014

Pasumai Avadi & Smart Centre

Launch of the Pasumai Avadi project (green initiatives). Ma Foi Smart Centre inaugurated at Avadi — an employability skills development training centre, in partnership with Tech Mahindra Foundation.

2017

Skillz on Wheelz

"Skillz on Wheelz" — a mobile digital literacy programme for women and girls — is launched, bringing digital skills training directly to communities at their doorsteps across Tamil Nadu.

Today

28+ Years of Sustainable Social Impact

The Foundation continues to expand its reach — 100,000+ lives impacted, 15,000+ youth skilled and trained, 10,000+ placed in sustainable careers, 8,000+ students supported through scholarships, 500+ women empowered through livelihood initiatives, 50+ CSR & development partners, 100+ training centres, and 25+ Centres of Excellence established (AI Labs, CoEs, Digital Labs, EV Labs).
