The Team

Dedicated professionals working every day on the ground — managing programmes, coordinating with communities, and ensuring every beneficiary is seen and heard.

Prabhakar L

Head — Operations

A graduate in Industrial Relations, Economics and Sociology from St. Joseph's College, Bangalore University with a Post-Graduation from Loyola College, Madras University, and MPhil from PRIST University. Prabhakar brings over 21 years of experience in network coordination, programme management, proposal development, outcome impact monitoring, and documentation.

He has worked across Climate Change, Disaster Risk Management, Livelihood, Social Enterprising, Corporate Relations, Education, Health, Counselling, and Social Research.

+91 7200359000 prabhakar@themafoifoundation.in

S.K. Thanigaivelan

Project Coordinator — Disha Sports Academy

A Post Graduate in Social Work from Mahalakshmi College of Arts and Science, Thanigaivelan was a basketball player and boxer during his school years, representing Chennai district and Tamil Nadu in championships. He has 7 years of experience as a Value Education teacher and School Counsellor, and has been with Ma Foi for 4+ years in charge of the Disha Sports Academy.

+91 9551262220

Vasanthabala

Project Officer — Gems of Disha

A Post Graduate from the renowned Madras School of Social Work, supplemented with 2 PG Diplomas in Business Management and HR. Vasanthabala has been with Ma Foi since 2008 and is in charge of the Gems of Disha project, which provides supplementary coaching and tools to academically weaker students.

+91 9551088592

Vidhyapathi. T

Project Officer — Disha Education

Holds a PG Diploma in Social Entrepreneurship from the Centre for Social Initiative Management (CSIM), Chennai, and a BA in Sociology from Tamil Nadu Open University. Vidhyapathi works as a Field Officer for the Disha Education initiative, connecting with families and scholars on the ground.

+91 9840837694

Sundari

Project Manager — Women Empowerment Programme

Sundari heads the Women Empowerment Programme at the Foundation, overseeing the Sornam Self Help Groups initiative across Chennai and Virudhunagar. She manages microfinance operations, vocational training coordination, and entrepreneurial support for over 60,000 women in the Foundation's network.

+91 8870475346
